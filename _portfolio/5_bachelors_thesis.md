My bachelor's thesis (2025, Charles University, supervisor Michal Pavelka) develops a novel approach to fluid–structure interaction (FSI) based on the Symmetric Hyperbolic Thermodynamically Compatible (SHTC) equations, discretized with Smoothed Particle Hydrodynamics (SPH). Since both the fluid and the solid are described by a single system of equations, the implementation and the coupling of the two phases become straightforward. The method is validated on a range of numerical tests, including the classical Turek–Hron FSI benchmark, an elastic beryllium plate, the lid-driven cavity, and flow past a cylinder. The full text is available as <a href="https://michaelmoncek.github.io/files/bachelor_thesis.pdf">PDF</a>.
