Student Faculty Grant project (supervisor Michal Pavelka) studying different SPH discretizations of the distortion field — the SHTC state variable representing the inverse deformation gradient. It compares the weighted least-squares projection of the distortion field itself with the projection of the velocity gradient, and relates both to the unified SHTC-SPH method. While mathematically equivalent in the continuum, one discretization turns out to be numerically unstable for the flow Ȧ = −AL while the other remains stable; this is verified numerically on the beryllium plate benchmark. The full report is available as <a href="https://michaelmoncek.github.io/files/particle_distortion_shtc.pdf">PDF</a>.
